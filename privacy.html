Privacy Policy

Maxylogy TikTok Studio

Last updated: August 28, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how Maxylogy TikTok Studio handles information when a user connects a TikTok account.

Information we process

When you connect TikTok, the application may receive basic TikTok profile information and authorization data required to provide the requested publishing features. Access tokens are used only to perform the authorized actions requested by the user.

How we use information

Information is used to authenticate the user, show basic account information, and provide TikTok content-upload and publishing functionality requested by the user.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is transmitted to TikTok only as necessary to provide the requested integration.

Data retention and deletion

Users may stop using the integration and revoke authorization through TikTok. Server-side authorization data should be deleted when no longer required for the service.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the service owner through the contact method provided with the application.
